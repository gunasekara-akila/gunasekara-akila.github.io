Professional Profile

I am a Civil & Structural Engineer with experience in the analysis, design and construction-stage review of reinforced-concrete, steel and water-retaining structures. My work combines structural calculations, technical reporting, drawing review, site inspection and multidisciplinary coordination.

I have contributed to local and international water-infrastructure projects, including the Rajshahi WASA Water Supply Project in Bangladesh, and I also undertake structural design packages for two-storey residential buildings.
